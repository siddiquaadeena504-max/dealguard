# DealGuard: 2:30 demo video script

One deal, end to end: **Notion's study-with-me sponsorship ($1,800)**. It's a USD deal, so the invoice is a real PayPal sandbox invoice.
Record at 1440×900 in light mode, with the cursor visible. Use the hosted Render link.

**Before recording**
- Open the hosted link once so the server is awake.
- In a second tab, log in to https://www.sandbox.paypal.com as your sandbox **Personal** (buyer) account.
- Free AI models can take 10–40 seconds on "Extract". Cut that wait out when editing.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:15** | Landing page. Let the hero preview loop once. | "Creators with 50,000 followers are their own finance team. They deliver the post, then chase brands for weeks to get paid. DealGuard is an AI agent that does the chasing for them." |
| **0:15–0:25** | Click **Try the live demo**. Dashboard loads; numbers count up. | "This is Aanya's dashboard. In one glance: what she's invoiced, what's paid, what's late, and whose usage rights are about to expire." |
| **0:25–0:35** | Hover the **Needs your attention** cards. | "The agent has already spotted two brands paying late, and a Nykaa ad running on rights that end in five days." |
| **0:35–1:00** | **New deal from email** → **Notion** (first in the inbox) → **Extract deal with AI**. Fields animate in; words highlight in the email. | "A new offer just landed from Notion. One click, and the AI pulls out the brand, the $1,800 fee, the two videos, the go-live date, net 45 terms and six months of usage rights. Every field shows how confident it is, and you can see where it came from." |
| **1:00–1:15** | Change **Contact email** to your sandbox Personal account's email → **Review invoice**. Pause on the preview. | "Nothing happens until Aanya approves. This is the exact PayPal invoice the brand will get." |
| **1:15–1:25** | **Approve & send** → success animation → deal page. | "Approved. The invoice goes out through PayPal's Invoicing API, and the deal is now being guarded." |
| **1:25–1:45** | Switch to the sandbox buyer tab and pay the invoice. Back in DealGuard, click **Refresh status** → confetti, status flips to **Paid**. | "The brand pays with PayPal. DealGuard checks PayPal, and it's paid. No chasing." |
| **1:45–1:55** | **Collaborators & splits**: add an editor at 15% → **Approve & send payouts**. | "Aanya's editor gets a 15% share, again only after she approves. In this sandbox demo the payout step is simulated." |
| **1:55–2:15** | Back to dashboard → open **Mamaearth** (late). Chase agent recommends **Firm** → **Draft** → show the email → **Approve & send**. | "For brands that pay late, the chase agent escalates from friendly to firm to final, and she approves every message." |
| **2:15–2:25** | Open **Nykaa** → countdown ring at 5 days → **Draft renewal pitch**. | "And before usage rights expire, it pitches a paid extension, so content is never used for free." |
| **2:25–2:30** | Back to the landing page with the shield. | "DealGuard. Every deal, guarded." |

**Tips:** turn off notifications. Keep the demo banner visible so it's clear payments are sandbox only. Use royalty-free music only (YouTube Audio Library), or none. Upload to YouTube as **Public**.
