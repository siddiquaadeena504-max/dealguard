# DealGuard: 2:30 demo video script

One deal, end to end: **GlowSkin's winter launch**. Record at 1440×900, light mode, with the cursor visible.
Before recording, add your PayPal sandbox keys so the invoice is real, and keep the sandbox **personal** (buyer) account logged in in a second tab.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:15** | Landing page. Let the hero preview loop once (email → extract → invoice → paid). | "Creators with 50,000 followers are their own finance team. They deliver the post, then chase brands for weeks to get paid. DealGuard is an AI agent that does the chasing for them." |
| **0:15–0:25** | Click **Try the live demo**. Dashboard loads; numbers count up. | "This is Aanya's dashboard. In one glance: what she's invoiced, what's paid, what's late, and whose usage rights are about to expire." |
| **0:25–0:35** | Hover the **Needs your attention** cards. | "The agent has already spotted two brands paying late, and a Nykaa ad running on rights that end in five days." |
| **0:35–1:00** | **New deal from email** → demo inbox → **GlowSkin** → **Extract deal with AI**. Fields animate in; words highlight in the email. | "A new offer just landed from GlowSkin. One click, and the AI pulls out the brand, the ₹1,50,000 fee, the Reel and three Stories, the go-live date, net 30 terms and 90 days of usage rights. Every field shows how confident it is, and you can see exactly where it came from." |
| **1:00–1:15** | Fix one field if you like → **Review invoice**. Pause on the preview. | "Nothing happens until Aanya approves. This is the exact PayPal invoice the brand will get, with a line for every deliverable." |
| **1:15–1:25** | **Approve & send** → success animation → deal page. | "Approved. The invoice goes out through PayPal's Invoicing API, and the deal is now being guarded." |
| **1:25–1:45** | Switch to the sandbox buyer tab, pay the invoice. Back in DealGuard, click **Refresh status** → confetti and shield pulse, status flips to **Paid**. | "When the brand pays, PayPal tells us through a webhook, or we check on demand. Paid. No chasing." |
| **1:45–2:00** | **Collaborators & splits**: add an editor at 15% → **Approve & send payouts**. | "Aanya's editor gets their 15% automatically through PayPal Payouts, again only after she approves." |
| **2:00–2:15** | Open **Mamaearth** (9 days late). Chase agent recommends **Firm** → **Draft** → show the email → **Approve & send**. | "For brands that pay late, the chase agent escalates from friendly to firm to final, and sends the reminder through PayPal." |
| **2:15–2:25** | Open **Nykaa** → countdown ring at 5 days → **Draft renewal pitch**. | "And before usage rights expire, it pitches a paid extension, so content is never used for free." |
| **2:25–2:30** | Back to the landing CTA with the shield. | "DealGuard. Every deal, guarded." |

**Tips:** turn off notifications; use Ctrl+K once to show the command palette if you have spare seconds; keep the demo banner visible to make it clear payments are sandbox only.
